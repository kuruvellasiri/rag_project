SAMPLE STUDENT HANDBOOK
Example policies for document-processing practice. These are fictional examples and are not official college rules.

Attendance
Example policy: Students are encouraged to attend all scheduled classes. An instructor may ask a student to discuss repeated absences.

Examinations
Example policy: Students should arrive before the published examination start time and bring their campus identification card. The examination office should publish permitted materials in advance.

Grading
Example policy: A course grade may be based on assignments, participation, and examinations. Instructors should explain the grading breakdown at the start of the course.

Student conduct
Example policy: Students are expected to treat classmates and staff respectfully and to avoid disrupting learning activities. Concerns may be reported to the student support office.

ID cards
Example policy: Students should carry their campus ID while on campus. A lost card should be reported to the campus services desk so the card can be replaced.

Library rules
Example policy: Library users should keep noise low, return borrowed items by their due dates, and follow staff instructions. Food and drink should be kept away from library materials.

Fees
Example policy: Students should review fee statements and payment dates issued by the finance office. Questions about a charge may be directed to that office.

Leave
Example policy: A student who needs leave should notify the appropriate academic office and follow the leave request steps published by the institution.

Campus safety
Example policy: Students should follow posted safety instructions, keep exits clear, and report hazards to campus security or facilities staff.
